about

i'm an artist and software engineer based in europe. i've been drawing since i was a kid and trained formally — i work in traditional and digital media across portraits, illustration, comics, and character design. my work is stylized, oriented around personality: what makes a subject distinctly themselves, what light reveals about them, how the space around them breathes.

artist statement

i make art about the ordinary — the faces, moments, and corners of life that pass without ceremony. i'm interested in what's beautiful about them, and in showing that through my own eyes. stylized, because the point is never a record of what was there. the point is what i saw.

available for commissions, illustration, and comics work.
