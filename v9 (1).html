05 Day 05 ✓ Mon, May 11
SITE ~20 min
DEPLOY TO GITHUB PAGES + POINT YOUR DOMAIN. Two steps: (1) In your repo on GitHub, go to Settings → Pages → set Source to 'Deploy from a branch', pick main (or master), folder / (root). Hit Save. GitHub gives you a yourname.github.io URL — click it to confirm it loads. (2) Point your domain: go to your registrar (Namecheap, Porkbun, etc.), find DNS settings. Add 4 A records pointing to: 185.199.108.153, 185.199.109.153, 185.199.110.153, 185.199.111.153. Add a CNAME record: www → yourusername.github.io. Back in GitHub Pages settings, enter your custom domain and check 'Enforce HTTPS'. In your repo root, create a file called CNAME (no extension) containing just your domain, e.g. yourname.com. DNS takes up to 24h to propagate — the .github.io URL works immediately. Update your Linktree and both bios with your domain.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Post 4–5×/week on each platform. Consistency beats perfection. A rough sketch posted beats a masterpiece hoarded.

Engage daily, even on rest days. Comments on others' work are your #1 growth lever at this size. Aim for 3–5 genuine interactions per day.

Engage UP and SIDEWAYS. Comment on accounts slightly bigger than yours (500–5K) and accounts your size. Big accounts rarely notice; peers become allies.

Reels are your discovery engine on IG. The algorithm pushes Reels to non-followers. Post at least 1/week.

On X, personality wins. Art + opinions + humor + vulnerability. Pure art accounts grow slow on X — being a person who makes art grows faster.

Pinterest is your silent salesperson. Unlike IG and X, Pinterest is a search engine — pins you create today drive traffic for months. Pin every new piece, write keyword descriptions that speak to collectors and interior designers, and let it compound in the background while you focus on community-building elsewhere.

📷 PREP tasks come the night before. Any post that needs footage or staged shots has an amber reminder the day before. Do it while you're already working — takes 2 minutes to set up a phone on a stand.

Your site is your home base. Green tasks build it one step at a time — none take more than 20 min. By end of month it's a real collector-ready destination.

Think like a collector magnet. Mix "I'm talented" posts (finished work) with "I'm a real person" posts (process, opinions, life). Collectors buy from people they feel connected to.
